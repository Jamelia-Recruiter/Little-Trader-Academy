🕯️ Candle Lab

Candle Detective

What does a candle tell us?

A candle is like a tiny story of a price during a chosen time. Its body shows where it opened and closed. Its wicks show the highest and lowest prices reached.

High
Body
Open ≈ Close

Patterns are clues, not promises. Traders look at the surrounding chart and wait for confirmation.

🌪️ Volatility World

Deriv learning zone

What is volatility?

Volatility describes how much a price moves. Imagine a bouncy ball: sometimes it moves gently, sometimes it jumps around a lot.

⚽〰️📈
More movement = higher volatility

Meet the Volatility family

Deriv has synthetic markets with names such as Volatility 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100. The number describes a volatility level in the index name. These are simulated markets, not ordinary company shares.

Some have faster movement than others. A fast-moving chart can be harder to understand, so start by learning slowly.

🔎 ICC Detective Mission

Indication → Correction → Continuation

ICC is a way to study how price may move in stages. First look for a clue, then a pullback, then see whether the original move continues. It is not a guaranteed signal.

💡

1. Indication

Price shows a possible direction, such as a strong move upward.

↩️

2. Correction

Price pulls back or pauses. We do not chase the first move.

➡️

3. Continuation

Look for signs that price is moving in the original direction again.
