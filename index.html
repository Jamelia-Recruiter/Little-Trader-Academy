🕯️ Candle Academy

Every candle tells a story about price movement.

Meet the two basic candles

GREEN
BULLISH 🐂
RED
BEARISH 🐻

🟢 Bullish

Price closed higher than it opened.

🔴 Bearish

Price closed lower than it opened.

Know the parts of a candle

🔝 High: Highest price reached.

🔻 Low: Lowest price reached.

🟣 Open: Price when the candle started.

🟡 Close: Price when the candle finished.

The thin lines are called wicks. The thick part is the body.

⚡ Volatility School

What is volatility?

Volatility describes how much and how quickly a price moves.

Imagine a roller coaster 🎢. Sometimes it moves gently. Sometimes it moves quickly!

Meet the indices

⚡ V10

Volatility 10 has a lower volatility setting than V25, V50, V75 and V100.

⚡ V25

A synthetic index with its own volatility setting.

⚡ V50

Study its candles and price movements.

⚡ V75

Learn to observe its price movements.

⚡ V100

Compare its movements with other indices.

⚡ 1HZ indices

Learn that these indices have a different tick frequency.

Important lesson 🛑

These numbers describe different volatility settings. They do not tell us whether the next candle will go up or down. No index guarantees a profit.
