🕯️ Candle Academy

Let's learn how candles tell the story of price!

Meet the two main candles

🟢 BULLISH
🔴 BEARISH

🟢 Bullish: price closed higher than it opened.

🔴 Bearish: price closed lower than it opened.

🧩 Candle body and wick

WICK + BODY

Top wick = highest price.

Bottom wick = lowest price.

Body = the space between opening and closing prices.

Open = where the candle began. Close = where it finished.

🚀 The ICC Mission

Three important steps. Let's become chart detectives!

ICC means:

1️⃣ I

INDICATION

Price shows a strong move or structure break.

2️⃣ C

CORRECTION

Price pulls back or retraces. We don't chase it.

3️⃣ C

CONTINUATION

We wait for rejection, a structure shift and candle-close confirmation.

🎮 Build your ICC sequence

Put the three steps in the correct order by choosing the next step.

🛑 When should we wait?

  • When price has not made a clear indication.
  • When the correction has not happened.
  • When there is no rejection or confirmation.
  • When price only spikes beyond a level and falls back.

Remember: a breakout alone is not enough.
